# Acme billing

Acme is billed monthly on the date you subscribed. We accept cards and UPI.

Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge — contact support and we
process the refund to your original payment method within 5 business days.

To cancel your subscription, go to Settings → Billing → Cancel. Cancelling
stops future charges; your plan stays active until the end of the current cycle.
